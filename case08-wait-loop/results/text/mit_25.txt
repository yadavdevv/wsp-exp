To find the number of boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we need to determine how much money she has left after calculating the costs of all the other items.

**Step 1: Calculate the cost of the milk.**
Marie ordered 5 packs of milk at $3 each.
$$ \text{Total cost of milk} = 5 \times \$3 = \$15 $$

**Step 2: Calculate the cost of the apples.**
Marie ordered 4 apples at $1.50 each.
$$ \text{Total cost of apples} = 4 \times \$1.50 = \$6.00 $$

**Step 3: Calculate the cost of the chicken meal.**
Marie ordered one chicken meal for $12.
$$ \text{Cost of chicken meal} = \$12 $$

**Step 4: Calculate the remaining money used by Marie.**
We start with her total payment of $50 and subtract the costs of the other food items.
$$ \text{Remaining money} = \$50 - (\$15 + \$6 + \$12) $$
$$ \text{Remaining money} = \$50 - \$33 $$
$$ \text{Remaining money} = \$17 $$

**Step 5: Calculate the number of pizza boxes.**
Let $x$ be the number of boxes of pizza. We know that each box costs $8.50.
The remaining money ($\$17$) must be exactly enough to pay for the boxes.
$$ 8.50x = 17 $$
$$ x = \frac{17}{8.50} $$
$$ x = \frac{170}{85} $$
$$ x = 2 $$

Marie ordered 2 boxes of pizza.
