$$ \text{Rate} = 2 \text{ cm/hr} $$

Multiply the rate by the duration to find the total length lost.
$$ \text{Total Melting} = \text{Rate} \times \text{Duration} $$
$$ \text{Total Melting} = 2 \text{ cm/hr} \times 4 \text{ hr} $$
$$ \text{Total Melting} = 8 \text{ cm} $$
