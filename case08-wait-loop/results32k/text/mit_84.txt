Let $W$ be the number of games won by the team.
Let $L$ be the number of games lost by the team.

We are given two pieces of information:
1.  The team played 22 games in total.
    $$W + L = 22$$
2.  The team won 8 more games than they lost.
    $$W - L = 8$$

To find the number of wins ($W$), we can set up a system of linear equations.
From the first equation, we can express $L$ in terms of $W$:
$$L = 22 - W$$

Substitute this expression for $L$ into the second equation:
$$W - (22 - W) = 8$$

Simplify the equation:
$$W - 22 + W = 8$$
$$2W - 22 = 8$$
$$2W = 8 + 22$$
$$2W = 30$$
$$W = 15$$

So, the team won 15 games.

From $W - L = 8$, we can express $L$ as $L = W - 8$.
Substituting this into the total games equation:
$$W + (W - 8) = 22$$
$$2W - 8 = 22$$
$$2W = 30$$
$$W = 15$$

Both methods yield the same result.
