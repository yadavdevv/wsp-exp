4.  **Result**: She has 23 post-it notes remaining overall.

We can set up an algebraic equation based on the total number of notes she started with and the notes she used.

Let $N$ be the number of notes in the package.
Let $R$ be the number of notes remaining.
Let $U$ be the number of notes used.

According to the problem:
*   She started with 80 notes.
*   She bought $N$ notes.
*   She used $U$ notes on the cups.
*   She ended with $R = 23$ notes.

Since the notes she placed on the cups must come from the package, the total notes she had at the beginning (80) plus the notes she bought ($N$) minus the notes she used ($U$) should equal the remaining notes ($R$).

However, there is a specific constraint implied by the phrasing "If she had 23 post-it notes remaining overall".
This phrasing implies that the notes in the purse (80) plus the notes in the package ($N$) are the sources of the remaining notes.

$$80 + N - U = R$$
$$80 + N - 220 = 23$$
$$N = 23 + 220 - 80$$
