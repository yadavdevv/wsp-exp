1.  **Identify the position of the first stop:**
    The problem states that Henry first stopped after **20 miles**. This means he traveled 20 miles from the start.
    $$ \text{Position}_{\text{first}} = 20 \text{ miles} $$

2.  **Identify the position of the second stop:**
    The problem states that his second stop was **15 miles before the end** of the trip.
    Since the total distance of the trip is 60 miles, we can determine the position of the second stop by subtracting the distance from the end of the trip.
    *   Note: "Before the end" implies a position relative to the end point. If the trip ends at mile 60, then 15 miles before the end would be at mile $60 - 15 = 45$.
    $$ \text{Position}_{\text{second}} = 45 \text{ miles} $$

3.  **Calculate the distance between the stops:**
    The distance between the first and second stop is the absolute difference between their coordinates.
    $$ \text{Distance} = | \text{Position}_{\text{second}} - \text{Position}_{\text{first}} | $$
    $$ \text{Distance} = | 45 - 20 | = 25 \text{ miles} $$

Thus, Henry traveled 25 miles between his two stops.
