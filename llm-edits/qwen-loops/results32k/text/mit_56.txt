*   **Ticket:** The problem states Peter "gets a ticket for $7". This implies that the cost to obtain a ticket is $7.
*   **Popcorn:** The problem states Peter "gets popcorn for $7". This implies that the cost associated with obtaining popcorn is $7.

Since the question asks how many times Peter can go to the movies, we assume he intends to attend a movie.
*   Total cost per movie = (Price of Ticket) + (Price of Popcorn)
*   Total cost per movie = $7 + $7 = $14.

**3. Calculate the Number of Movies:**
*   Peter has a total budget of $42.
*   Using the formula: $\frac{\text{Total Budget}}{\text{Cost per Movie}} = \text{Number of Movies}$
*   Number of Movies = $\frac{42}{14} = 3$.
