Let's determine the guesses made by each friend based on the number of jelly beans in the jar.

**Step 1: Analyze the first friend.**
The first friend said, "80."
So, the initial number is **80**.

**Step 2: Analyze the second friend.**
The second friend said, "20 more than half the first one."
We need to calculate half of the first number (80) and then add 20.
$$ \text{Half of } 80 = 80 \div 2 = 40 $$
$$ 40 + 20 = 60 $$
So, the second friend guessed **60**.

**Step 3: Analyze the third friend.**
The third friend said, "25% more than the first one."
This means we take 25% of the first number (80) and add it to the original number. Alternatively, we can use the formula $x \times (1 + \text{percentage})$.
$$ 25\% \text{ of } 80 = 80 \times 0.25 = 20 $$
$$ 20 + 80 = 100 $$
Or using multiplication:
$$ 80 \times 1.25 = 100 $$
So, the third friend guessed **100**.

**Step 4: Calculate the average guess.**
Now we have three guesses: 80, 60, and 100. To find the average, we divide the sum by the count (which is 3).
$$ \text{Sum} = 80 + 60 + 100 = 240 $$
$$ \text{Average} = 240 \div 3 = 80 $$
