    $$ \text{Contemporary} = 20 \times 0.20 = 4 \text{ students}. $$

3.  **Determine the Number of Remaining Students**:
    Subtracting the students in contemporary dance from the total gives the number of students remaining in the class.
    $$ \text{Remaining} = 20 - 4 = 16 \text{ students}. $$

4.  **Calculate the Number of Students in Jazz Dance**:
    The problem states that **25%** of the remaining students enrolled in jazz dance.
    $$ \text{Jazz} = 25\% \text{ of } 16 = 0.25 \times 16 = 4 \text{ students.} $$

5.  **Calculate the Number of Students in Hip-Hop Dance**:
    The problem states that the **rest** of the students enrolled in hip-hop dance. Assuming the remaining students are composed of the two other groups (contemporary and jazz), the number of students in hip-hop is the remainder of the total class.
    $$ \text{Hip-Hop} = \text{Total} - (\text{Contemporary} + \text{Jazz}) $$
    $$ \text{Hip-Hop} = 20 - (4 + 4) = 20 - 8 = 12 \text{ students.} $$

    $$ \text{Hip-Hop} = 16 \text{ (from step 3)} - 4 = 12 \text{ students.} $$
