2.  The cost per blue tie is $40.

$$\text{Number of ties} = \frac{\text{Total Cost}}{\text{Cost per tie}}$$
$$B = \frac{200}{40} = 5 \text{ ties}$$

So, John bought 5 blue ties.

**Step 2: Analyze the Red Ties**
The problem states: "John buys twice as many red ties as blue ties."

$$\text{Number of red ties} = 2 \times B = 2 \times 5 = 10 \text{ ties}$$

$$\text{Price per red tie} = \text{Price per blue tie} \times (1 + 0.50)$$
$$\text{Price per red tie} = 40 \times 1.5 = 60 \text{ dollars}$$

$$\text{Price per red tie} = 40 + (0.50 \times 40) = 40 + 20 = 60 \text{ dollars}$$

*   Cost of Blue Ties = $5 \text{ ties} \times \$40/\text{tie} = \$200$
