2.  **Calculate the number of new potted plants received:**
    *   Mary received **18** new potted plants.
    *   These are added to her total collection.

3.  **Calculate the total number of plants Mary originally possessed:**
    *   Total = Existing plants + New plants
    *   Total = $80 + 18 = 98$ potted plants.

4.  **Determine the number of plants Mary will remove:**
    *   She intends to give **1** potted plant from each ledge.
    *   There are 40 ledges, so she will remove $40 \times 1 = 40$ plants.

5.  **Calculate the number of plants Mary will remain with:**
    *   Remaining = Total original plants - Plants removed
    *   Remaining = $98 - 40 = 58$.
