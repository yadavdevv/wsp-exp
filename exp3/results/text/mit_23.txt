The problem states that the candle melts **2 centimeters** for every **hour** it burns.
$$\text{Rate} = 2 \text{ cm/hour}$$

**Step 2: Determine the duration of the burning period.**
We need to calculate the time elapsed between 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM.

*   End time: 5:00 PM

$$5:00 \text{ PM} - 1:00 \text{ PM} = 4 \text{ hours}$$
So, the candle burns for **4 hours**.

**Step 3: Calculate the total amount shortened.**
Multiply the rate by the duration:
$$\text{Total Centimeters Shortened} = \text{Rate} \times \text{Hours}$$
$$\text{Total Centimeters} = 2 \text{ cm/hour} \times 4 \text{ hours}$$
$$\text{Total Centimeters} = 8 \text{ cm}$$
