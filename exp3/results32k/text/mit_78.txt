**1. Analyze the Options:**

*   **Option A:** Buy flowers in packages of 3.
    *   Price per package: $2.50
    *   Packages per flower: 3
    *   Cost per flower: $\frac{2.50}{3}$

*   **Option B:** Buy flowers in packages of 2.
    *   Price per package: $\$1$
    *   Packages per flower: 2
    *   Cost per flower: $\frac{1}{2}$

**2. Determine the Total Cost for 18 Flowers:**

We need to calculate the total cost for the exact number of flowers Vincent wants to acquire, which is 18.

*   **Using Option A (Packages of 3):**
    *   Number of packages = $\frac{18 \text{ flowers}}{3 \text{ flowers/package}} = 6 \text{ packages}$
    *   Total Cost = $6 \times \$2.50 = \$15.00$

*   **Using Option B (Packages of 2):**
    *   Number of packages = $\frac{18 \text{ flowers}}{2 \text{ flowers/package}} = 9 \text{ packages}$
    *   Total Cost = $9 \times \$1.00 = \$9.00$

**3. Calculate Savings:**

The amount saved is the difference between the higher cost and the lower cost.
*   Total Cost (Option A) = $15.00
*   Total Cost (Option B) = $9.00
*   Savings = $\$15.00 - \$9.00 = \$6.00$

Alternatively, if you view the cost per flower directly:
*   Cost of 18 flowers in Option A: $18 \times (\frac{2.50}{3}) = 15$
*   Cost of 18 flowers in Option B: $18 \times (\frac{1}{2}) = 9$
*   Difference: $15 - 9 = 6$
