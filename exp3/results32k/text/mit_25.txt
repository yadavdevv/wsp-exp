To find the number of boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we can break down the costs of every item she purchased and compare the total amount she paid against the total budget.

**Step 1: Calculate the cost of each item individually.**

*   **Chicken Meal:** Marie ordered one meal, which costs $\$12$.
    *   Cost = $\$12$
*   **Milk Packs:** There are 5 packs, and each costs $\$3$.
    *   Cost = $5 \times \$3 = \$15$
*   **Apples:** There are 4 apples, and each costs $\$1.50$.
    *   Cost = $4 \times \$1.50 = \$6$
*   **Pizza Boxes:** Let $x$ be the number of boxes. Each box costs $\$8.50$.
    *   Cost = $8.50x$

**Step 2: Calculate the total cost of the known items.**

We add the costs of the items identified above to find the total spent on these items:
$$ \text{Total Cost} = \$12 + \$15 + \$6 = \$33 $$

**Step 3: Determine the remaining amount available for purchases.**

Marie paid a total of $\$50$. We subtract the cost of the known items ($\$33$) from the total paid ($\$50$) to find out how much money was left over for the pizza boxes.
$$ \text{Remaining Amount} = \$50 - \$33 = \$17 $$

**Step 4: Calculate the number of pizza boxes.**

Now, we divide the remaining amount by the cost of one box to find the number of boxes.
$$ \text{Number of Boxes} = \frac{\text{Remaining Amount}}{\text{Cost per Box}} $$
$$ \text{Number of Boxes} = \frac{17}{8.50} $$
$$ \text{Number of Boxes} = 2 $$

Since the result is an integer, Marie ordered exactly two boxes of pizza.
