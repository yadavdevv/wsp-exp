$$ \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 1}} = 60 $$

**Step 2: Find the downloads in the second month.**
The problem states that the number of downloads in the second month was three times as many as the downloads in the first month.
$$ \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 2}} = 3 \times \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 1}} $$
$$ \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 2}} = 3 \times 60 = 180 $$

**Step 3: Find the downloads in the third month.**
The problem states that the downloads were reduced by 30% in the third month. This means we start with the downloads from the second month and subtract 30%.
$$ \text{Reduction} = 30\% \text{ of } \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 2}} = 0.30 \times 180 $$
$$ 0.30 \times 180 = 54 $$
So, the remaining downloads in the third month is:
$$ \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 3}} = \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 2}} - \text{Reduction} $$
$$ \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 3}} = 180 - 54 = 126 $$
Alternatively, you can calculate the remaining percentage directly: $100\% - 30\% = 70\%$.
$$ \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 3}} = 180 \times 0.70 = 126 $$

**Step 4: Calculate the total number of downloads over the three months.**
$$ \text{Total Downloads} = \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 1}} + \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 2}} + \text{Downloads}_{\text{month 3}} $$
$$ \text{Total Downloads} = 60 + 180 + 126 $$
$$ \text{Total Downloads} = 366 $$
