To solve this problem, we need to determine the total earnings Lloyd makes weekly based on his daily production rate and the selling price per dozen.

**Step 1: Determine the number of dozens produced per day.**
We are given that Lloyd produces **252 eggs** per day.
A "dozen" is a standard unit equal to **12 eggs**.
To find out how many dozens are produced in one day, we divide the total number of eggs by the number of eggs in a dozen:
$$ \text{Number of dozens per day} = \frac{252 \text{ eggs}}{12 \text{ eggs/dozen}} $$
$$ \text{Number of dozens per day} = 21 \text{ dozens} $$

**Step 2: Calculate the earnings per day.**
The problem states that Lloyd sells his products for **$2 per dozen**.
To find the total earnings, we multiply the number of dozens produced daily by the price per dozen:
$$ \text{Daily earnings} = 21 \text{ dozens} \times \$2 \text{/ dozen} $$
$$ \text{Daily earnings} = \$42 $$

**Step 3: Calculate the earnings for a week.**
Since there are **7 days** in a week, we multiply the daily earnings by 7 to find the total earnings for the week.
$$ \text{Weekly earnings} = \$42 \text{ (daily)} \times 7 \text{ (days)} $$
$$ \text{Weekly earnings} = 294 $$
