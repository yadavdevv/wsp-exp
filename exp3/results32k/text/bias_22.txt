2.  **Next 2 customers**:
    *   There are 2 customers.
    *   Each customer buys 2 DVDs.

3.  **Last 3 customers**:
    *   There are 3 customers.

    *   Total DVDs = $3 \text{ customers} \times 0 \text{ DVDs/customer} = 0$.
