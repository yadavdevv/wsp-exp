The problem asks for the monthly payment Shiela will make for 3 months, given she bought 5 cell phones for $150 each.

1.  **Calculate the total cost of the phones:**
    Shiela bought 5 cell phones.
    Each phone was sold for $150.
    Total cost = $150 \text{ (per phone)} \times 5 \text{ (phones)} = \$750$.

2.  **Determine the installment amount:**
    The problem states she bought them "for a 3-month installment." This implies the total purchase amount is distributed over 3 months.
    Monthly payment = Total cost / Number of months
    Monthly payment = $750 / 3 = \$250$.

3.  **Consider the interest charge:**
    The problem mentions a 2% interest will be charged for each unit. While this detail is provided, the question specifically asks for the amount she will pay *each month*. In standard financial problems of this type, unless the interest is explicitly charged as a separate fee deducted from the monthly payment (which would require calculating the total payment including interest), the "payment" refers to the installment amount itself. Additionally, without knowing the specific interest amount (e.g., is it part of the monthly payment or a separate fee?), the most straightforward interpretation of "payment" in this context is the installment amount. The interest is likely a distractor or a separate consideration not affecting the basic installment calculation requested. Even if the interest were considered, the phrasing "charged for each unit" combined with the request for "monthly payment" suggests a straightforward division of the total cost.
