To find the number of pieces taken by the guests, we can break down the information given in the problem statement:

1.  **Calculate the total number of pieces originally baked.**
    Grandma Jones baked 5 apple pies.
    Each pie was cut into 8 pieces.
    To find the total number of pieces, multiply the number of pies by the number of pieces per pie:
    $$5 \text{ pies} \times 8 \text{ pieces/pie} = 40 \text{ pieces}$$

2.  **Identify the remaining pieces.**
    After the guests had eaten their pieces, there were 14 pieces remaining.
    We can represent this as:
    $$\text{Remaining pieces} = 14$$

3.  **Determine how many pieces were taken.**
    The number of pieces taken (or consumed) is the total number of pieces minus the number of pieces remaining.
    $$\text{Pieces Taken} = \text{Total Pieces} - \text{Remaining Pieces}$$
    $$\text{Pieces Taken} = 40 - 14$$
    $$\text{Pieces Taken} = 26$$

Thus, the guests took 26 pieces of pie.
