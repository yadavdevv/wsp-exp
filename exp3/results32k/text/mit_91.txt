Let $M$ represent the number of pets Marcia has.

$$C = 4$$

**Step 2: Determine Marcia's pets.**
The problem states: *"Marcia has two more pets than Cindy."*

$$M = C + 2$$

$$M = 4 + 2$$
$$M = 6$$

**Step 3: Determine Jan's pets.**
The problem states: *"Jan has three times the number of pets as Marcia."*
This can be written as:
$$J = 3 \times M$$

$$J = 3 \times 6$$
$$J = 18$$
So, Jan has 18 pets.

$$\text{Total} = J + M + C$$
