Let $D$ represent Darrell's age and $A$ represent Allen's age.

**Step 1: Understand the relationship between the ages.**
We are told that Darrell and Allen's ages are in the ratio of 7:11. Therefore, we can write the following equation based on their current ages:
$$ \frac{D}{A} = \frac{7}{11} $$
Multiplying both sides by 11 gives us:
$$ 11D = 7A \quad \text{(Equation 1)} $$

**Step 2: Use the information about the total age.**
The problem states that their total age now (in the present) is 162 years old.
$$ D + A = 162 \quad \text{(Equation 2)} $$

**Step 3: Solve for Allen's age ($A$).**
From Equation 1, we can express $D$ in terms of $A$:
$$ D = \frac{7}{11}A $$
Substitute this expression for $D$ into Equation 2:
$$ \frac{7}{11}A + A = 162 $$

To combine the terms on the left side, find a common denominator (which is 11):
$$ \frac{7A}{11} + \frac{11A}{11} = 162 $$
$$ \frac{18A}{11} = 162 $$

Multiply both sides by 11 to isolate $A$:
$$ 18A = 162 \times 11 $$
$$ 18A = 1782 $$

Divide by 18:
$$ A = \frac{1782}{18} $$
$$ A = 99 $$

So, Allen's current age is 99.

**Step 4: Calculate Allen's age 10 years from now.**
The question asks for Allen's age 10 years from now.
$$ \text{Future Age} = \text{Current Age} + 10 $$
$$ \text{Future Age} = 99 + 10 $$
$$ \text{Future Age} = 109 $$
