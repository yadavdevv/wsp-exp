To determine how much Lloyd makes on eggs per week, we need to calculate the total number of eggs produced in a week and then multiply that by the price per dozen.

**Step 1: Determine the number of eggs produced per day.**
The problem states that his chickens produce **252 eggs per day**.

**Step 2: Determine the number of weeks in a year or a week.**
There are 7 days in a week. We will calculate the number of eggs produced in one week.
$$ \text{Weekly Eggs} = 252 \text{ (eggs/day)} \times 7 \text{ (days/week)} $$
$$ \text{Weekly Eggs} = 1764 \text{ eggs} $$

**Step 3: Determine the number of dozens produced per week.**
Lloyd sells the eggs for \$2 per dozen.
First, we convert the total number of eggs into dozens.
$$ \text{Number of Dozens} = \frac{\text{Total Eggs}}{12 \text{ (eggs/dozen)}} $$
Alternatively, we can view 1 dozen as 12 eggs.
$$ \text{Number of Dozens} = \frac{1764}{12} $$
$$ \text{Number of Dozens} = 147 $$

**Step 4: Calculate the revenue (money made).**
Lloyd sells the dozens for \$2 per dozen.
$$ \text{Weekly Revenue} = 147 \text{ dozens} \times \$2 \text{ per dozen} $$
$$ \text{Weekly Revenue} = \$294 $$
