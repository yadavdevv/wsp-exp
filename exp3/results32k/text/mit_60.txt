The problem asks for the number of good oranges in a basket containing 25 oranges. We are given the following information:
1.  There is **1** bad orange.
2.  There are **20%** unripe oranges.
3.  There are **2** sour oranges.
4.  The remaining oranges are described as good.

To determine the number of good oranges, we first identify the counts of the other categories.

**Step 1: Determine the number of bad oranges.**
From the problem statement, explicitly stated: "**1** is bad".
So, the number of bad oranges, denoted as $n_{bad}$, is 1.

**Step 2: Determine the number of unripe oranges.**
The problem states "**20%** are unripe". Assuming this refers to the total population of the basket (since percentages generally apply to the entire group unless specified as a ratio of a subset), we calculate:
$$ \text{Number of unripe oranges} = 20\% \times 25 $$
$$ \text{Number of unripe oranges} = 0.20 \times 25 = 5 $$
So, there are 5 unripe oranges.

**Step 3: Analyze the "sour" oranges.**
We are told there are "**2** are sour".
Assuming "sour" and "unripe" are distinct attributes (unless stated otherwise, e.g., that all sour oranges are unripe, which would contradict the count of 5 unripe), we treat them as separate categories.
Thus, the number of sour oranges, denoted as $n_{sour}$, is 2.

**Step 4: Calculate the number of good oranges.**
Let $n_{good}$ be the number of good oranges.
The total number of oranges is the sum of the parts identified:
$$ n_{bad} + n_{unripe} + n_{sour} + n_{good} = 25 $$
Substituting the known values:
$$ 1 + 5 + 2 + n_{good} = 25 $$
$$ 8 + n_{good} = 25 $$
$$ n_{good} = 25 - 8 $$
$$ n_{good} = 17 $$

Alternatively, using the complementary logic:
$$ \text{Total} = 25 $$
$$ \text{Bad} = 1 $$
$$ \text{Unripe} = 5 $$
$$ \text{Sour} = 2 $$
$$ \text{Good} = 25 - 1 - 5 - 2 = 17 $$

Note: The term "20% are unripe" implies that the unripe subset has a count of 5. Since "unripe" and "sour" are distinct physical traits (or at least distinct categories), the counts are additive.

Therefore, there are 17 good oranges.
