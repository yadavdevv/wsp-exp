*   **Purchase Price:** $\$80,000$
*   **New Value (Market Value):** $\$120,000$

$$ \text{Profit} = \text{New Value} - \text{Purchase Price} $$
$$ \text{Profit} = 120,000 - 80,000 $$
$$ \text{Profit} = 40,000 $$
