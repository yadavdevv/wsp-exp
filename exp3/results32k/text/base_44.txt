**Step 1: Determine the amount of beeswax required.**

$$ \text{Pounds of beeswax} = \frac{\text{Number of candles}}{\text{Candles per pound}} $$
$$ \text{Pounds of beeswax} = \frac{20}{10} = 2 \text{ pounds} $$

**Step 2: Calculate the total cost of the supplies.**
The problem states that one pound of beeswax and the wicks cost **$10.00**. Since we determined that we need 2 pounds of beeswax to make 20 candles, we multiply the pounds by the cost per pound.

$$ \text{Total Cost} = 2 \text{ pounds} \times \$10.00/\text{pound} = \$20.00 $$

**Step 3: Calculate the total revenue from sales.**
The problem states that each candle sells for **$2.00**. With 20 candles being sold, we calculate the total earnings.

$$ \text{Total Revenue} = 20 \text{ candles} \times \$2.00/\text{candle} = \$40.00 $$

**Step 4: Calculate the net profit.**
Net profit is the difference between the total revenue and the total cost of supplies.

$$ \text{Net Profit} = \text{Total Revenue} - \text{Total Cost} $$
$$ \text{Net Profit} = \$40.00 - \$20.00 = \$20.00 $$
