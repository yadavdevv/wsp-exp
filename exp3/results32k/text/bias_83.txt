To find the total number of thorns, we need to calculate the total number of roses first, and then multiply by the number of thorns in one rose.

**Step 1: Calculate the total number of roses.**
Dan plants 3 rose bushes, and each bush has 25 roses.
$$3 \text{ bushes} \times 25 \text{ roses/bush} = 75 \text{ roses}$$

**Step 2: Calculate the total number of thorns.**
Each rose has 8 thorns. We multiply the total number of roses found in Step 1 by the number of thorns per rose.
$$75 \text{ roses} \times 8 \text{ thorns/ros} = 600 \text{ thorns}$$

There are 600 thorns in total.
